1. Who we are

Vonera Private Limited (“Vonera,” “we,” “us”), registered at Somajiguda, Hyderabad, Telangana 500082, India, builds proposal and costing software for pharmaceutical CDMO and CRO bid desks. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

This policy covers two different things, and they are not the same: this marketing website (vonera.in), and the Vonera platform used by our customers’ teams to manage proposals. Section 2 covers the former; Sections 3 onward cover the latter.

2. This website

vonera.in is a static marketing site. As of the date above, it does not:

  • use cookies or any similar tracking technology;
  • run analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party trackers;
  • contain any form that collects your data;
  • store anything in your browser (no localStorage or session data).

The only way this site can receive your information is if you click a “Book a walkthrough” or “Get a custom quote” link and choose to send us an email. That opens your own email application; nothing is transmitted to us until you actually send the message. If you do, we receive whatever your email contains — typically your name, email address, and the content of your message — and handle it as described in Sections 4 and 8.

If this changes — for example, if we add analytics or a contact form in future — we will update this section and, where required, ask for your consent first.

3. The Vonera platform

When a pharma CDMO or CRO customer uses Vonera, we process personal data belonging to that customer’s own team — the people who log in and use the platform. This typically includes:

  • Account data — name, work email, role, and login credentials of each user your organisation authorises.
  • Activity data — who created, edited, or approved a given proposal, and when, as part of the platform’s version history and audit trail.
  • Content you upload — synthesis routes, costing data, and proposal documents, which may themselves contain the names of your scientists, reviewers, or customer contacts.

We do not collect personal data about your end customers unless it arrives embedded in the documents your team uploads (for example, a named contact on a customer’s RFQ).

4. How we use data

We use the data described above to:

  • provide and maintain your account and the platform itself;
  • generate the proposals, cost tables, and reports you ask the platform to produce;
  • maintain the version history and audit trail described on our governance page;
  • respond to support requests and correspondence you send us;
  • meet legal and contractual obligations, including responding to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use customer chemistry or business data to train any AI model, whether ours or a third party’s.

5. Legal basis

Under the DPDP Act, we process personal data on the basis of:

  • Consent — where you or your organisation have agreed to it, such as when creating a platform account;
  • Contract — where processing is necessary to provide the service your organisation has signed up for;
  • Legitimate use — for example, responding to an email you voluntarily send us.

You (or, for platform accounts, your organisation’s administrator) can withdraw consent at any time, subject to Section 9.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with:

  • Infrastructure providers — the Vonera platform runs on enterprise AWS infrastructure, which stores and processes data on our behalf under its own security and confidentiality obligations to us.
  • Authorities — where required by law, such as a valid court order.

Access to a customer’s data within Vonera is scoped to that customer’s own authorised users. Vonera staff do not hold a standing login inside a customer’s tenant; any staff access is an exceptional, logged step taken only where necessary (for example, to resolve a support issue), consistent with what is described on our Governance & IP page.

7. Security

We apply the following safeguards to platform data:

  • encryption in transit and at rest;
  • row-level isolation between customer tenants, so one customer’s data is not reachable from another’s session;
  • role-scoped access, limited to each user’s function within their own organisation;
  • a full version history and activity trail on every revision.

No system is completely immune to compromise. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

8. Retention

We retain platform data for as long as your organisation’s account with us is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, or contractual obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Emails sent to us through this website are retained only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your query, unless you become a customer, in which case ordinary business-record retention applies.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, as a data principal you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent was the basis for processing;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity;
  • Grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer (Section 10), and subsequently the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details in Section 13. If you use the platform through an employer account, some requests may need to go through your organisation’s administrator, since they are the party that authorised your account.
