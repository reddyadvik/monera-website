The platform

8 capabilities. One system.

01 Generation — Drop in the route, get the costed table back

Nexus reads the chemistry and generates the full RD table — every stage, reagent and quantity laid out, with yields and stoichiometry derived rather than re-typed.

  • Hours of manual data entry turn into a single review-and-approve step.
  • Chemical identities resolved and cross-checked against public references.
  • The route feeds the cost engine directly — no re-keying between chemistry and price.
A full day of table-building becomes a short review.
02 Workflow — Every department, working at once

The single biggest driver of turnaround is the department relay. Nexus removes it — R&D, Manufacturing, Quality, Supply Chain and EHS all work the same live proposal in parallel.

  • Teams work simultaneously — more bids out per quarter without adding headcount.
  • Nothing stalls unseen in an inbox — every hand-off is visible.
  • Governed approvals keep the speed without giving up control.
The department relay disappears — teams work at once, not in line.
03 Costing — A number you can defend line by line

Nexus replaces fragile spreadsheets with one deterministic engine — so when a client pushes back you can re-quote on the spot without losing the thread, or the margin.

  • Re-quote 100 kg → 1,000 kg in seconds, target margin held intact under client pressure.
  • Every figure traces back to the chemistry, so it stands up to a client or an auditor.
  • Sanity checks flag impossible values before they reach the quote.
Margin computed to your target, on every tier — not guessed.
04 Manufacturing — Model the plant, cost every operation

Manufacturing economics are where quotes quietly go wrong. Nexus lets you lay out the actual plant — reactors, utilities, unit-ops — and cost every operation instead of estimating it.

  • Cost the plant you will actually run, stage by stage — not a round-number guess.
  • Utility and labour rates from your company profile, so every estimator prices the same way.
  • Per-batch operation costs flow straight into the quote.
Every reactor, utility and unit-op — costed per batch, not estimated.
05 Chemistry search — Search your archive by molecule

Your proposal history is a chemistry asset most CROs can't use. Nexus lets you search it the way a chemist thinks — by structure, not by keyword.

  • Draw a molecule and find every past proposal that contains that substructure.
  • Reuse a proven route or price instead of re-quoting from scratch.
  • Verified structures, not fragile name-matching.
Search your entire proposal archive by molecule, not keyword.
06 AI assistant — Ask your pipeline anything

The answers to run your business are buried in files. Nexus surfaces them in plain English, straight from your live data.

  • Ask "what's our win rate?" and get a real answer, not a search result.
  • Every answer cites its source, so you can trust the number.
  • Onboard a new estimator faster — the system already knows your history.
Answers from your live data — with sources.
07 Analytics — See every bottleneck, while you can still fix it

Nexus turns your whole pipeline into live analytics — company-wide and department by department.

  • Win rate, proposal quality and stage-by-stage progress on one dashboard.
  • Per-department turnaround shows exactly where bids stall — before they cost a deadline.
  • Spot the bottleneck this quarter, not in the post-mortem.
Turnaround and win-rate, live and per department.
08 Output — A finished, client-ready proposal

A complete, well-structured, on-brand document — assembled and governed, not stitched together the night before.

  • A full multi-section proposal, AI-drafted and approved section by section.
  • Your voice and detail, kept under control per section.
  • Exported to branded PDF or Word in one click.
A branded, multi-section proposal — client-ready in one export.
