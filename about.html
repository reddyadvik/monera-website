About Vonera

Software built for one industry, not one client

Our team

The people building it

Founder

Advik Reddy Bommineni

Founder of Vonera, building the platform after a track record of founding ventures and civic initiatives.

Read more →

Advik Reddy Bommineni is the Founder and Director of Vonera. Alongside Vonera, he founded Solus India and leads the Vikranti Foundation, a registered NGO delivering food security and essential-needs support to underserved communities.

His background spans venture development, product design and applied data work — from leading entrepreneurship and innovation initiatives to AI research on machine learning applications in cancer-treatment planning, and startup feasibility and pricing work for early-stage ventures. That combination of technical grounding, commercial thinking and hands-on execution shapes how he approaches building Vonera.

Co-Founder

Nomula Venkat Reddy

Co-Founder of Vonera, drawing on a background across pharmaceutical science, presales and project management.

Read more →

Nomula Venkat Reddy is a pharmaceutical industry professional with a background across drug discovery, CROs, CDMOs and API manufacturing, holding master’s degrees in both science and business administration. With a foundation in organic chemistry and formal expertise in operations and project management, he has built and led high-performing presales and proposal management functions.

As Co-Founder of Vonera, he leads development of the platform’s AI-powered automation for API cost estimation, manufacturing process design, proposal management and end-to-end workflow — replacing fragmented manual processes with one system built to help pharmaceutical teams respond faster and more competitively.

Advisors

Chief Advisor

Mahender Rao Siripragada

Veteran R&D and CDMO leader, guiding Vonera’s product and industry strategy.

Read more →

Mahender Rao Siripragada has worked at the intersection of chemistry, drug development, technology transfer, commercial manufacturing, project management and regulatory affairs — building the R&D and CDMO capabilities that take a molecule from laboratory concept to a reliable, market-ready product. His career spans leadership roles at Dr. Reddy’s, Orchid, Panacea Biotec, Alembic and Neuland, and a Ph.D. in Synthetic Organic Chemistry from IICT grounds work across API R&D, process development, technology transfer, GMP manufacturing and CDMO strategy.

From Principal Scientist at Dr. Reddy’s to President of R&D at Neuland, he has repeatedly built systems around science — taking promising chemistry and making it reproducible, scalable, compliant and commercially sustainable. Today he advises pharmaceutical and CDMO organisations, including Vonera, on R&D strategy, API and NCE development, and process and technology transfer.
